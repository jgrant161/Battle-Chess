Privacy Policy

Checkmate & Chivalry · Effective October 29, 2026

Checkmate & Chivalry is a chess game. We built it to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app chat. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

What stays on your device

The game saves your game in progress and your settings (sound and battle animations on or off) in your device's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network use

The game plays fully offline. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that need data, such as online play, we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
