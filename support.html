Checkmate & Chivalry Help

How do I move?

Tap one of your pieces. Its legal moves light up. Tap a lit square to move there. Red corners mark a capture, which starts a fight.

Can I skip the fights?

Tap the fight to skip it, or turn fights off with the Battles button.

How do I play against the computer?

Tap New game, choose Vs computer, pick your army and a strength: Squire (easy), Knight (medium) or King (hard).

What does Undo do?

It takes back the last move. Against the computer it takes back your move and the computer's reply.

Does it save my game?

Yes. Your game in progress is saved on your device after every move and comes back when you reopen the app.

Rules questions

The game follows standard chess rules, including castling, en passant, pawn promotion, stalemate, the fifty-move rule, threefold repetition and draws by insufficient material.
